Case 01 · news videos with an AI presenter · a content team

The news broke this morning. Your video is out by lunch.

A system that turns a news story into a finished presenter video in a day. It finds the angle, writes the script, films an AI presenter, edits, checks, and hands you the file for a yes.

The problem

News moves in hours. Creators and influencers move in days: briefing, filming, revisions, invoices. By the time the video lands, the story is old. Every extra video costs the same again, and you cannot scale people the way you can scale a presenter who never sleeps.

What was built

A chain of steps that run one after another. A fresh sweep of the hottest news. A script in the brand’s voice, three options, you pick one. The presenter video generated from the script with an avatar tool, same face and setting every time. The edit assembled from approved scene types with captions. Checks for timing, look and sound, then a second AI review. Your final yes.

1news sweepthe hottest stories right now, with the detail nobody noticed
2scriptthree scripts in your voice; you pick one
3presentergenerated from the script with an avatar tool; same face, same setting
4editapproved scene layouts, captions, your brand
5checkstiming, look and sound, then a second AI review
6your yesnothing goes out without it

What changes: a video a day instead of one a week, the same presenter and quality every time, and a cost per video that stops growing with the number of videos.

Case 03 · research agent · for writers and news teams

Every news story hides a stranger story. This agent finds it first.

An agent that reads what nobody has time to read: the full article, the comments, the data. It brings back the angle that stops a scroll, with a source for every fact.

The problem

Headlines are the same on every channel. The video that gets shared opens on the detail nobody else found, and finding it by hand costs a writer half a day.

What was built

A hunt in six steps: the whole article, the comments under it, the live data, the knock-on effects, and a map of who is affected. The agent proposes several angles, scores them against each other, and picks the strongest hook. Every fact comes with its source. The script step cannot start without the research file.

1readthe whole article, not the headline
2read the commentswhat readers noticed and journalists missed
3check the datalive numbers behind the story
4map the effectswho is affected, and how
5score the ideasthe agent scores several ideas and picks the best
6hand overthe angle, with a source for every fact

What changes: writers start from the best angle, not from a blank page; nothing goes on screen without a source. The same agent can watch a market, a competitor or a topic for any team.

Case 04 · shared knowledge system · in use

Nobody on your team starts a script from a blank page again.

Send the agent any reel, link or screenshot. It takes the piece apart, files what makes it work, and later writes hooks, calls to action and scripts from patterns that already worked. For the whole team.

The problem

Inspiration dies in screenshots. The best writer’s taste leaves with them. New people start from zero.

What was built

The agent transcribes a clip and breaks it into its parts: hook, story structure, visuals, text, sound, call to action. It turns the specifics into a reusable pattern, removes duplicates and files it. The library mirrors to Notion, so the whole team reads it and adds to it. Ask for five hooks and it writes them from what worked.

Case 05 · growth research agent · built as a working sample

Every growth channel you never had time to research. Researched.

An agent that studies every marketing channel from four sides: the channel, the audience, the rivals, your own assets. It writes a plan with a kill rule. People keep every public action.

The problem

A team plans growth around the three channels it already knows. The others stay unresearched, so the plan is a guess with a budget.

What was built

Built for a hiring interview: a growth plan for a software product across every channel, in days. A board of 104 areas and channels. Each goes through gated stages: assess, research, analyse, plan, design the system, run in shadow mode. A second agent checks every stage. A weak plan goes back. When the agent needs data it was not given, it stops and says so.

1assessthe goal and the one metric it improves
2researchthe channel, the audience, the rivals, your assets
3analysepatterns, with a critic checking each claim
4planthree to five tactics, each with a stop rule
5designone agent per job, a person at every gate
6shadow runthe agent drafts; a person scores them

What changes: a researched plan for every channel, not only the familiar ones; drafts queued for people to post; nothing posted by the agent.

Case 06 · monitoring agent · a content team

Know what is about to go viral in your niche before it does.

An agent that checks the accounts you follow three times a day, spots the posts speeding up, and sends the team the top ten.

The problem

Scrolling feeds by hand finds what already peaked. The team reacts to yesterday.

What was built

A run that reads the accounts, re-measures every recent post, works out how fast each one grows against its own normal, and flags the ones breaking out. The top ten go to the team’s chat. A public board shows the rest. Self-checks run first; a failed check stops the run.

1readthe accounts you follow, three times a day
2re-measureevery recent post, again
3scorehow fast it grows against its own normal
4alertthe top ten to the team’s chat
5publisha public board

What changes: the team posts into a rising wave instead of after it, and nobody scrolls for a living. The code is open: mr-board on GitHub.
