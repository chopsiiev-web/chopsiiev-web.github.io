03 The problem

Repeat work does not look expensive. It looks like Tuesday.

  • The one person who knows the process gets sick.Everything waits. The steps were never written down, so nobody can pick them up.
  • The report is late again.The numbers lived in four tools and one inbox. Someone spent Friday copying them.
  • The same mistake comes back every month.The fix was a message in a chat, not a rule. Nobody remembers it in week three.
  • The AI tools are paid for and idle.They answer questions. Nobody turned them into something that does the job without being asked.
  • The default answer is a hire.Then the new person does the same repeat work, and the cost is permanent.
  • Growth stalls at the size of the team.Every new channel, market or product needs hands you do not have.
04 How I build

Case 01 · video production system · a content team

The reel engine: a news story in, a finished presenter video out.

Finds the story, writes the script, films an AI presenter, edits it, checks every step, and will not move on until each step is proven. A week per video became a day.

a week became a day9 forced edit steps70 checks per videonothing posts on its own

The job

Short vertical news videos for social, every week, in the brand’s style, with one consistent presenter.

The problem

An outside studio took a week per video. Quality swung from one to the next. Every fix was made by hand and forgotten, so the same faults came back.

What was built

One chain from news to delivery. A research step that finds the strange side-effect of a story and keeps a source link for every fact. A script step with a hook checklist the system cannot skip; a person picks one of three. An AI presenter filmed in short takes, each checked word by word against the script, with a test take approved before the rest are paid for. An edit step with six scene templates and nine forced stages, each saving a record. A second AI review that can block, a send guard, and a person’s final yes.

a failed step goes back to its cause, never forward

Three videos through the one command; the last one 59 seconds, 20 scenes, 70 checks green. Turnaround from a week to a day. Nothing is posted without a person.

Case 03 · research agent · for scripts and news

The news researcher: one story in, the strange side-effect out, every fact with a link.

Reads the whole article, the top comments and the data behind a news story, then ranks 6 to 8 angles by how hard they stop a scroll. Scripts cannot start without its file.

6 to 8 scored anglesa source link per factfour lensesrefuses stale research

The job

Give writers a concrete, surprising angle on a news story, fast, with proof behind it.

The problem

A video that opens on the headline gets scrolled past. Finding the strange detail took hours of reading, and facts went unchecked.

What was built

A five-step hunt: full articles read to the end, the top comments, free live data, keyword sweeps for physical consequences, then a cause-and-effect map through four lenses: things, money, power, ordinary people. Candidates are scored on five criteria. The file carries a source link for every fact and a log of every judgment call. The script step refuses to run without a fresh file.

1read to the endthe strange details live in the lower half
2mine the commentswhat readers noticed and journalists missed
3pull the dataship, flight, price and trend feeds, labelled live or lagging
4map cause and effectfour lenses, disagreements recorded
5score the angles6 to 8 candidates, five criteria
6file ita source link for every fact, one log line

Three stories hunted, 7 to 8 angles each, winners scored 42 to 44 of 50. A claim that went bad a day later was caught and reworded. The same build can watch a market, a competitor or a topic for any team.

Case 04 · shared knowledge system · in use

The marketing library: a team memory of what works, and it writes.

Send it any reel, link or screenshot. It takes the piece apart, files the reusable pattern, and later writes hooks, calls to action and scripts from patterns that already worked. Built so a whole team can read it and add to it.

34 reel breakdowns70 hook patterns16 call-to-action patternsmirrored to Notion

The job

Keep every good hook, structure, format and call to action from the feed in one place, reusable by anyone on the team, so nobody writes from a blank page.

The problem

Inspiration dies in screenshots. Nobody re-reads a swipe file. A new writer starts from zero, and the best writer’s taste leaves with them.

What was built

A skill that transcribes a clip and breaks it into twelve parts: packaging, the first three seconds, structure, pacing, visuals, on-screen text, sound, stats, the call to action, why it works, how to recreate it, how to remix it. Specifics become a pattern with slots; duplicates are merged; a one-file index is rebuilt; a drift check goes red when the index, the log or the Notion mirror fall behind. On request it writes options from proven patterns, each with the pattern it used and a one-line why.

The loop that gets smarter

103 files in 14 sections, 34 full reel breakdowns, 70 hook patterns, 16 call-to-action patterns, mirrored to Notion and backed up every 10 minutes. The goal: everyone feeds it what they see working, and everyone writes from proven patterns.

Case 05 · growth research agent · built as a working sample

A growth researcher for every channel, from four angles at once.

For each of 104 marketing areas and channels, an agent researches the channel, the audience, the rivals and the product’s own assets, then writes a plan with a kill rule. A second agent checks every stage. People keep every public action.

104 areas and channels4 angles each90 stage gates passednever posts, pays or messages

The job

A researched growth plan for a software product across every channel, in days, built as a working sample for a hiring interview.

The problem

Growth planning by hand covers the three channels someone already knows. The other forty are never researched, so the plan is a guess with a budget.

What was built

A board of 104 cells. Each moves through gated stages: assess, research, analysis, strategy, system design, shadow run. Four angles per channel: how the channel works, who the audience is in their own words, how rivals show up, and what the product already has. A critic challenges every claim; a play scored under 7 of 10 goes back; a stage without proof blocks the next. When it needs data it was not given, it stops and says so instead of guessing.

2researchchannel, audience, rivals, own assets; 20+ winning posts saved
3analysiscounts, not impressions; a critic challenges each claim
4strategy3 to 5 plays with a kill rule; under 7 of 10 goes back
5system designone agent per job, budgets, human gates
6shadow rundrafts queued, scored; a person blind-grades ten

90 stage gates passed over two nights of unattended runs, 24 clean stops for missing access, and a queue of drafts for people to post from. Nothing went public on its own.

Case 06 · monitoring · a content team

The trend radar: sees which posts are taking off, three times a day.

Reads 14 accounts morning, noon and evening, scores each post against its own normal pace, and sends the top ten risers to the team’s chat. A public board shows the rest.

181 runs in 102 days3 runs a day, no operator29 self-checks per runopen source

The job

Tell a content team what is rising in their niche before it peaks, without anyone scrolling feeds.

The problem

Scrolling by hand three times a day finds what already peaked. The team reacted to yesterday.

What was built

A run that reads the accounts through a logged-in browser, re-measures every post under 72 hours old, works out pace and heat, labels what is breaking out, sends the top ten to Telegram once each, and publishes a board. Twenty-nine checks of the scoring maths run first; a failed check aborts the run.

1read14 accounts, 25 newest posts each
2re-measurelikes, views, replies for every post under 72 h
3scorepace and heat against the post’s own normal
4alerttop ten risers to the team’s chat, once each
5publisha public board, sorted by pace

181 runs in 102 days without an operator, 41,000 growth snapshots, a public board. The code is open: mr-board on GitHub.

Case 07 · writing system · in use

Three scripts per story, each with an opening line that passed ten checks.

A news story goes in. Three short-video scripts come out, each a different angle, each opening line stress-tested: five tries, six checks, one “so what?” test. The checker refuses a script without its proof.

3 scripts per storya hook under 12 wordsa 10-row checklist14 refusals built in

The job

Scripts a presenter can shoot the same day, with proof behind every line and an opening that stops a scroll.

The problem

Scripts came out long, with stale numbers, no proof of research and weak openings. Advice in a document did not stop any of it.

What was built

A fixed shape, a word band of 144 to 192, no digits in spoken lines, a hook under 12 words, a ten-row scorecard with real evidence in every row, and a checker that runs at the end of every session and refuses a script with a row missing. Only a person can waive it, in writing.

Nine news scripts in three batches; the approved one went straight into production. The quality no longer depends on anyone’s discipline that day.
